About me

Hi, I'm Tjorven.

I'm a creative developer studying Digital Design & Development at Devine (Howest, Kortrijk).

Code is where I feel most at home. I like building things people play with: projection-mapped installations, scroll-driven stories, 3D scenes and experiments with machine learning, finished with motion that feels right.

I have a background in visual design as well, so I sweat the typography, spacing and timing, not just the logic underneath.
