About me

Hi, I'm Tjorven.

I'm a Digital Design & Development student at Devine (Howest, Kortrijk), somewhere right between a developer and a designer.

I like taking an idea all the way: from a rough sketch, to a polished design in Figma, to a working experience that feels good to use. On the code side that means WebGPU worlds, installations and motion; on the design side identities, editorial layouts and promo films.

Doing both means I design with how things will be built in mind, and I build with an eye for typography, timing and detail.
