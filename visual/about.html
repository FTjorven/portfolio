About me

Hi, I'm Tjorven.

I'm a visual designer studying Digital Design & Development at Devine (Howest, Kortrijk).

I care about typography, colour and layout, and about work with a clear point of view. I like taking a brand from first sketches and moodboards to a full identity: logo, type, photography, packaging and the app it lives in.

I also write code, so I design with how things will actually be built in mind, and I can hand over files a developer is happy to receive.
